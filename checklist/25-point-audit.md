# The 25-point local contractor website audit

Used for our published benchmark. Score honestly; every point should cite something you observed and dated.

## Part A - Business basics (/10)
1. Real, operating local business (2)
2. Website loads at its main URL (2)
3. Contact method present and working (phone and/or form) (2)
4. Decision-maker identifiable (owner/principal named publicly) (2)
5. Evidence of recent activity (reviews, recent work, updated copyright) (2)

## Part B - Visibility & mobile gaps (/10)
6. Loads correctly on a 390x844 mobile render (2)
7. Viewport meta present on all templates (1)
8. Tap-to-call present outside decorative sliders (2)
9. All primary CTAs resolve (no 404s on Request/Estimate/Quote buttons) (2)
10. Contact form exists where promised (1)
11. TLS certificate valid and hostname-matched (2)

## Part C - Fit & feasibility (/5)
12. The core problem is expressible in one sentence (1)
13. Fix is practical within a standard sprint (1)
14. Matches web/lead-capture services (1)
15. Timeline realistic (1)
16. No disqualifier present (1)

## Disqualify immediately (score 0, do not contact)
- Business permanently closed
- No contact path or identifiable decision-maker
- Corporate chain with centralized marketing control
- Legal/advertising restrictions prevent the work

## Bands
20-25 Top priority · 14-19 Good · 8-13 Watchlist · below 8 Skip

Rules we hold ourselves to: verify on rendered mobile, date every finding, never publish a stat you did not measure, and share findings about a named business with that business before publishing anything identifying them.
