Missed-call text-back: what it is, and what it must include

A missed call is a customer who tried to reach you and got nothing.

Text-back is the mechanism that answers them automatically — with compliance built in first.

The flow

  • Detect: an unanswered call on the primary business line
  • Respond: an automated text within seconds — templated, accurate, no promises
  • Log: every exchange recorded against the contact
  • Route: the conversation lands where a human sees it

Full playbook: missed-call text-back notes.

Compliance first — non-negotiable

  • 10DLC registration before any automated traffic
  • STOP honored immediately and permanently — one request ends the sequence forever
  • Quiet hours respected
  • Anything beyond templated replies gets human approval before sending

Where it fits

Text-back comes after the call path works. It cannot rescue a site where the phone button does not dial, or a number that renders as untyped plain text.

Order of operations: loads → dials → form sends → then automate the follow-up.
