Five website problems that cost contractors work

Findings from a rendered audit of 21 local home-services websites, October 2026. Published: DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23710476. Dataset: benchmark sheet. Businesses anonymized as P01-P21.

1. The site does not load at all

4 of 21 sites were unreachable or serving error content at their main address — on the root domain, not an obscure subpage. A maps listing that points to a dead site converts every impression into a lost call. Check yours on a phone, from outside your network.

2. The form everyone is told to use does not exist

8+ of 21 sites had a contact-capture defect. The clearest pattern: a contact page instructing visitors to "fill out the form below" while the page source contains zero form elements. Verify by submitting your own test entry — then confirm it actually arrives.

3. The phone number cannot be tapped

Phone numbers rendered as plain text, or a sole tap-to-call affordance buried inside a rotating hero slider. On a phone, a number you must copy-paste is a number most customers will never dial.

4. Certificate warnings on the customer's screen

5 of 21 sites served invalid or mismatched TLS certificates. Browsers now intercept the visit with a warning screen. Customers never report it — they just assume the business is not legitimate.

5. Demo content live for real customers

2 of 21 sites were still serving template placeholder copy — including a plumber whose "emergency repairs" page was titled for a different trade. Migrations and theme swaps leave demo content live because nobody re-reads the live site.

What to do with this

Run the four-point check — loads, dials, form sends, no warnings — on your own site tonight, on your actual phone. Date what you find. Defects move. The full scoring rubric and open-source fixes are in our public repository.

Findings vs recommendations: the counts above are measured results of the published audit. What to fix first is our operating judgment, not a study result — the audit measured defect presence, not revenue impact.
